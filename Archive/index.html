Our Mission

Plywood is an open marketplace for small thrift stores to display their good online. Plywood will show you what an item is worth, where it is located, and help you get in contact with a store so your treasure is ready for you when you arrive to pick it up. Alternatevely, you can also contact a store an inquire about shipping services through our system. Join today and start scavenging!
